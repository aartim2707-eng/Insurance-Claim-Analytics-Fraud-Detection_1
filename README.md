# Insurance Claim Analytics & Fraud Detection Project

**Tagline:** Turning claim data into insights for fraud prevention, efficiency, and customer trust.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project leverages SQL to analyze insurance claim data, detect fraud, evaluate customer experience, and measure operational efficiency.  
The goal is to transform raw claim and policy data into actionable insights that improve profitability, reduce risks, and enhance customer trust.

---

## 🔑 Key Features
- **Fraud Detection:** Identified fraudulent claims, frequent fraudsters, and ranked high‑risk claims by amount.
- **Customer Experience:** Analyzed settlement delays, ranked claims by processing time, and highlighted dissatisfaction drivers.
- **Policy & Claim Integration:** Detected mismatches between policy and claim statuses, ensuring data consistency.
- **Financial Impact:** Compared premiums vs. claims per customer, flagged loss‑making accounts, and calculated net payouts.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Measured employee performance, ranked claim officers by speed, and tracked escalation patterns.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Analyzed claim ratings to identify areas of improvement and success stories.

---

## 🛠️ SQL Techniques Used
- Joins for integrating customer, policy, and claim data  
- Aggregations (`SUM`, `COUNT`, `AVG`) for financial and operational metrics  
- Window functions (`RANK()`, `PARTITION BY`) for fraud and performance ranking  
- Case statements for risk categorization and profitability flags  
- Date functions (`DATEDIFF`, `ROUND`) for settlement delay analysis  

---

## 📌 Business Insights
- Fraud detection reduces financial losses and flags repeat offenders.  
- Settlement delay analysis highlights bottlenecks impacting customer trust.  
- Policy vs. claim mismatch detection ensures data integrity.  
- Premium vs. claim ratio analysis supports profitability decisions.  
- Employee performance tracking improves operational efficiency.  
- Claim ratings reveal satisfaction levels and areas needing improvement.  

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Strengthen fraud detection with predictive analytics and automated alerts.  
- Reduce settlement delays by streamlining approval workflows.  
- Integrate customer, policy, and claim data for a single source of truth.  
- Balance premium pricing with claim ratios to ensure profitability.  
- Improve customer satisfaction through faster resolution and transparency.
